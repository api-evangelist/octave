## Therapy Options
- [Individual Therapy](https://www.findoctave.com/individual-therapy): A personalized approach to mental health, focusing on individual needs and goals.
- [Couples Therapy](https://www.findoctave.com/couples-therapy): Therapy designed to help couples improve their relationship dynamics and communication.
- [Family Therapy](https://www.findoctave.com/family-therapy): A therapeutic approach that involves family members to address issues affecting the family unit.
- [In-Person Therapy](https://www.findoctave.com/in-person-therapy): Traditional face-to-face therapy sessions available for those who prefer in-person interactions.
- [DBT](https://www.findoctave.com/coe-dbt): Dialectical Behavior Therapy, a specialized form of cognitive-behavioral therapy aimed at helping individuals manage emotions and improve relationships.
